Verdict

A verdict for every position, not a pile of curves

Each position gets PASSMARGINALFAIL from its own compound's kinetics. Running a few degrees hot inside the compound's reversion window is marginal, not a failure — so you stop rejecting good cures.

Section map

See exactly where on the tyre

Results are drawn on your own tyre section, top and bottom mould. Layers without a thermocouple — belt skim, insulation, bead wire coat — are estimated from the positions around them and judged too.

Root cause

Press, compound or recipe — before anyone touches the cycle

Three independent signals are cross-checked for every compound zone: what the rubber needs (batch MDR vs its norm), what the press delivered, and top/bottom symmetry.

  • Here: bottom bead under-cured and 17 °C colder than the top → hardware. Check bladder condensate and the steam line, don't add cure time.
  • Whole zone short on an even press with a normal batch → recipe / cycle.
  • Batch TC90 far from its norm → material: investigate the mix.
Compound DNA

How it works

Built around the thermocouple procedure your lab already follows. No new hardware.

01

Describe the test

Tyre, press and mould, and the four cure timings: cure start, HPS hold, deflation, total. The engine cross-checks them against the thermocouples and flags a stopwatch that disagrees.

02

Drop the logger file

The raw HIOKI LR8400 export, as it comes off the logger. Pick the positions you recorded on the tyre section; dead or spiking channels are caught before the analysis.

03

Read the report

R&D engineers get the full engineering report. Operators get a plain verdict and a one-click Cure Record PDF for the test file.

What's inside

Everything a curing engineer checks by hand, done the same way on every test.

Cure equivalent & margins

Arrhenius cure equivalent at press open and deflation, ECM and time-at-temperature per position, against each compound's own floor.

Reversion-aware grading

A reversion index from the compound's MDR reversion times tells hot-but-fine from genuinely over-cured.

Root-cause tree

Hardware, material, recipe or local thermocouple seating — with the action to take, per compound zone.

Questions engineers ask first

Does our data leave the plant?

No. The engine is one HTML file that runs in the browser on your own PC. Logger files, compounds and recipes are never uploaded anywhere.

Which loggers are supported?

HIOKI LR8400-series exports work out of the box. Other loggers (Datapaq, Yokogawa, Graphtec, CSV exports) can be added: send a sample file.

Can it use our tyre sections, positions and compound codes?

Yes. Positions, components, compound specs, estimated layers and the section drawing are configuration. We set them up from your thermocouple procedure.

Where does the compound kinetics come from?

From your own MDR tests at several temperatures (activation energy, reversion times), or from a standard constant per tyre type if you prefer.

Is this validated?

It was built and validated on real production cure tests inside a tyre plant's material-development department, against the lab's own calculations.

Can we run our own cure test in the demo?

No. The public demo only replays its two built-in tests on synthetic data, so nothing in it can be configured or reused. Your own logger files, compounds and spec limits run in a pilot with the full engine, on your PC.
